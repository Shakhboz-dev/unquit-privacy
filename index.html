Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 24-noyabr

Ushbu maxfiylik siyosati «Unquit» ilovasi (Android va Windows versiyalari; bundan keyin — «Ilova») foydalanuvchilarning ma'lumotlariga qanday munosabatda bo'lishini tushuntiradi. Ilova yordamida siz kunlik vazifalar, odatlar, maqsadlar, eslatmalar, kelajakka maktublar, seriya, fokus vositalari, motivatsion videolar va ingliz tili darslarini boshqarasiz.

Qisqacha

Unquit deyarli barcha ma'lumotlaringizni faqat qurilmangizda saqlaydi; akkaunt yo'q, reklama yo'q, kuzatuv (analytics) yo'q. Internet orqali faqat SIZ harakat qilganingizda ma'lumot ketadi: (1) «Muammo haqida xabar berish» orqali yuborgan matningiz, (2) ilova qulagandan keyin rozilik bersangiz — texnik xatolik hisoboti, (3) ingliz tili mashqida bayroqchani bosib yuborgan mashq hisobotingiz (javobingiz, jumladan gapirish mashqida tanilgan nutq matni bilan), (4) «Hissa qo'shish» bo'limida ixtiyoriy yozgan ism va izohingiz — bularning barchasi Cloudflare Worker orqali developerga Telegram'ga boradi; shuningdek (5) bir martalik promo kod kiritganingizda, kod va anonim qurilma identifikatori tekshirish uchun shu xizmatga yuboriladi. Bundan tashqari uchta tashqi xizmat o'z siyosati bilan ishlaydi: Google Play (Plus obunasi, ixtiyoriy hissa, yangilanish tekshiruvi), telefoningizning nutqni tanish xizmati (ingliz bo'limidagi ixtiyoriy gapirish mashqlari — ovozni internet orqali qayta ishlashi mumkin) va havolasini o'zingiz bosgan pochta yoki Telegram ilovasi. Unquit ovozni yozib olmaydi, saqlamaydi va olmaydi.

Biz to'plamaydigan ma'lumotlar

Ilova quyidagilarni so'ramaydi va to'plamaydi:

  • ism, familiya, telefon raqami yoki email manzil («Hissa qo'shish» bo'limida o'zingiz ixtiyoriy yozgan ismdan tashqari — 4-bo'lim);
  • ro'yxatdan o'tish yoki tizimga kirish (akkaunt talab qilinmaydi);
  • joylashuv (GPS) ma'lumotlari;
  • ovoz yozuvlari;
  • kontaktlar, rasmlar yoki boshqa shaxsiy fayllar (siz o'zingiz tanlagan motivatsion videodan tashqari — 2-bo'lim);
  • to'lov kartasi ma'lumotlari (to'lovlarni Google Play amalga oshiradi — 4-bo'lim).

Qurilmada saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Siz Ilovaga kiritgan va Ilova siz uchun hisoblagan barcha ma'lumotlar faqat qurilmangiz xotirasida saqlanadi va developerning hech qanday serveriga yuborilmaydi:

  • vazifalar, odatlar, maqsadlar xaritalari, eslatmalar, kelajakka maktublar, va'da-shartnomalar, statistika va sozlamalar;
  • seriya (streak), muz zaxirasi, nishonlar va haftalik yakunlar;
  • qadam sanagich yoqilgan bo'lsa — telefon sensoridan olingan kunlik qadamlar soni (faqat son, joylashuvsiz);
  • ingliz tili bo'limidagi o'quv jarayoningiz (o'rganilgan so'zlar, takror jadvali, XP);
  • motivatsion video uchun siz tanlagan video faylning nusxasi (ilovaning o'z papkasida; videoni o'chirsangiz, nusxa ham o'chadi);
  • ilova paroli va maxfiy savol-javob (faqat qurilmada; biz ularni bilmaymiz va tiklay olmaymiz);
  • Plus obunasi va promo kod holati (tasdiq keshi).

Ma'lumotlar nusxasi (eksport) faqat siz buyurganingizda yaratiladi va siz tanlagan joyga (clipboard yoki fayl) yoziladi — uni qayerda saqlash va kimga berish sizning qo'lingizda.

Istisnolar — faqat 4-bo'limda sanab o'tilgan, sizning harakatingiz bilan yuboriladigan xabarlar (muammo xabari, xatolik hisoboti, mashq hisoboti, ixtiyoriy hissa izohi va promo kod tekshiruvi).

Telefoningizda Google zaxirasi yoqilgan bo'lsa, Android ilova ma'lumotining nusxasini Google akkauntingizda saqlashi mumkin. Buni telefon sozlamalarida o'chirish mumkin.

Ilova ruxsatlari

Ilova quyidagi ruxsatlardan foydalanadi (Android):

  • Bildirishnomalar — vazifa, odat, eslatma va maktublarni belgilangan vaqtda ko'rsatish, «Streak xavfda» va haftalik yakun xabarlari hamda fon xizmatlarining (fokus-sessiya, qadam sanagich, budilnik) ko'rinib turishi uchun;
  • Aniq vaqtli signal (alarm) — eslatmalar va ertalabki budilnik aynan to'g'ri vaqtda ishlashi uchun;
  • Budilnik va qulflangan ekran (to'liq ekranli bildirishnoma, ekranni uyg'otish va ohang fon xizmati) — faqat ertalabki budilnikni yoqsangiz, belgilangan vaqtda qulflangan ekranni uyg'otib ohang chalish uchun; ixtiyoriy ravishda batareya optimizatsiyasidan chiqarish so'ralishi mumkin (hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi);
  • Qurilma qayta yoqilganda ishga tushish — telefon o'chib-yonganda rejalashtirilgan eslatmalar va budilnik yo'qolmasligi uchun;
  • Vibratsiya — bildirishnoma kelganda, budilnik chalinganda va tugma bosilganda titrash uchun;
  • Jismoniy faollik — faqat siz biror odatda avtomatik qadam sanashni yoqsangiz so'raladi: telefon sensoridan qadamlar soni o'qiladi va qurilmada saqlanadi; fon xizmati sensorga ulanib turadi. Joylashuv ishlatilmaydi;
  • Foydalanish statistikasiga kirish (Usage access) va Boshqa ilovalar ustida ko'rsatish — faqat «Ilovalarni bloklash» vositasini yoqsangiz: blok davomida hozir qaysi ilova ochiq ekani tekshiriladi va tanlangan ilova ustiga to'siq oynasi chiziladi. Qaysi ilovalar ochilgani hech qayerda saqlanmaydi va yuborilmaydi; o'rnatilgan ilovalar ro'yxati faqat bloklanadiganlarni tanlashingiz uchun o'qiladi;
  • «Bezovta qilinmasin» rejimiga kirish — faqat Fokus-Qalqon yoqilganda: vazifa taymeri paytida telefonni jim qilib, keyin qaytaradi;
  • Mikrofon — faqat ingliz bo'limining gapirish mashqlarida, siz mikrofon tugmasini bosganingizda: nutqingizni telefoningizning nutqni tanish xizmati (masalan, Google) taniydi — imkon bo'lsa telefonning o'zida, bo'lmasa o'sha xizmatning serverlarida, uning o'z maxfiylik siyosati asosida. Ilova ovozni yozib olmaydi, saqlamaydi, o'zi olmaydi va hech kimga bermaydi; tanilgan matn javobingizni telefonda tekshirish uchun ishlatiladi va faqat siz o'zingiz mashq xatosi haqida hisobot yuborsangiz hisobot tarkibida ketadi. Mashqlarni bo'lim menyusida o'chirish yoki ruxsatni telefon sozlamalarida olib qo'yish mumkin;
  • Tarmoq holati — gapirish mashqidagi xato sababini («internet yo'q») aniqlash uchun; hech narsa yubormaydi;
  • Internet — faqat (a) «Muammo haqida xabar berish» xabari, (b) siz rozilik bergan xatolik hisoboti, (v) siz yuborgan ingliz mashqi hisoboti, (g) ixtiyoriy hissa izohi, (d) bir martalik promo kodni tekshirish hamda (e) Google Play yangilanishlari, Plus obunasi va hissa to'lovlari uchun.

Motivatsion video tanlash telefonning o'z fayl tanlagichi orqali bo'ladi — galereyaga ruxsat so'ralmaydi, Ilova faqat siz tanlagan bitta faylni oladi.

Internet, mikrofon (yuqorida aytilganidek, ovozni telefonning nutqni tanish xizmati o'z serverlariga yuborishi mumkin) va Google Play bilan bog'liq holatlardan boshqa hech bir ruxsat shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni yig'ish yoki uzatish uchun ishlatilmaydi.

Windows versiyasida ruxsatlar tizimi yo'q: ma'lumotlar kompyuterdagi ilova papkasida saqlanadi; gapirish mashqlari Windows'ning o'z nutqni tanish xizmatidan foydalanadi (uning onlayn tanishi Windows sozlamalaringizga bog'liq).

Internet va tashqi xizmatlar

Ilova asosan oflayn ishlaydi — barcha shriftlar, kurs va kontent ilova ichiga joylangan, kundalik foydalanish uchun internet talab qilinmaydi.

Internet faqat quyidagi holatlarda ishlatiladi:

  • Agar «Muammo haqida xabar berish» bo'limidan xabar yuborsangiz — yozgan matningiz yuborilgan vaqt, ilova tili va versiyasi hamda qurilma turi (masalan Android) bilan birga Telegram xizmati orqali developerga yuboriladi. Bu faqat siz «Jo'natish»ni bosganingizda sodir bo'ladi; internet bo'lmasa, xabar yuborilmaydi va internet yoqilguncha qurilmangizda turadi.
  • Agar ilova kutilmaganda yopilib qolsa — keyingi ochilishda sizdan texnik xatolik hisobotini (qurilma turi, tizim versiyasi va xatolik izi; shaxsiy mazmuningizsiz) yuborishni so'raymiz. Bu faqat siz «Yuborish»ni bosganingizda va internet yoniq paytda yuboriladi; internet o'chiq bo'lsa hisobot qurilmangizda qoladi, o'zingiz rad etsangiz — o'chiriladi.
  • Agar ingliz tili mashqida xato haqida bayroqcha tugmasini bosib hisobot yuborsangiz — mashq identifikatori, savol, sizning javobingiz (gapirish mashqida — tanilgan nutq matni), tanlagan sababingiz hamda ilova va OT versiyasi Telegram orqali developerga yuboriladi. Bu faqat siz yuborishni bosganingizda sodir bo'ladi; internet o'chiq bo'lsa hisobot qurilmada navbatda turadi.
  • Agar «Hissa qo'shish» bo'limida to'lovdan so'ng ixtiyoriy ravishda ism va izoh yozib yuborsangiz — o'sha ism, izoh va hissa darajasi Telegram orqali developerga yuboriladi.
  • Agar bir martalik promo kod kiritsangiz — kodning haqiqiyligini tekshirish, qayta ishlatilishining oldini olish va shu qurilmada tiklash imkonini berish uchun promo kod hamda anonim qurilma identifikatori (Android'da ANDROID_ID, Windows'da MachineGuid) developer boshqaradigan Cloudflare Worker xizmatida tekshiriladi va saqlanadi.
  • Agar email yoki Telegram havolasini o'zingiz bossangiz — tegishli tashqi ilova (pochta yoki Telegram) ochiladi.
  • Ilova Google Play orqali o'rnatilgan bo'lsa — ochilganda Google Play orqali yangi versiya bor-yo'qligini tekshiradi va siz rozilik bersangiz yangilanishni fonda yuklab oladi. Bunda sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz Google Play'ga yuborilmaydi; bu Google Play'ning standart yangilash mexanizmi bo'lib, unga Google'ning o'z maxfiylik siyosati qo'llaniladi.
  • Unquit Plus obunasi (oylik, 3 oylik yoki yillik) va «Hissa qo'shish» bo'limidagi ixtiyoriy to'lov Google Play orqali amalga oshiriladi. To'lov va karta ma'lumotlarini faqat Google qayta ishlaydi — Ilova va developer ularni ko'rmaydi; Ilova Google Play'dan faqat «obuna faol / faol emas» holatini oladi va uni qurilmada keshlaydi. Bu jarayonga Google Play'ning o'z shartlari va maxfiylik siyosati qo'llaniladi.
  • Agar ingliz bo'limidagi ixtiyoriy gapirish mashqlaridan foydalansangiz — ovozingizni telefoningizning nutqni tanish xizmati (masalan, Google) taniydi. Ilova undan oflayn ishlashni so'raydi, lekin ovoz telefondan chiqadimi-yo'qmi — shu xizmat hal qiladi: oflayn ingliz tili o'rnatilmagan bo'lsa (ba'zan o'rnatilgan bo'lsa ham), u ovozingizni internet orqali o'z serverlariga, o'z maxfiylik siyosati asosida yuborishi mumkin. Ovoz developerga yuborilmaydi, ilova uni yozib olmaydi va saqlamaydi. Mashqlar faqat mikrofonga ruxsat berganingizdan keyin ishlaydi va ularni bo'lim menyusida o'chirish mumkin. So'zlarni ovozli o'qish (talaffuz) telefonning o'z matnni o'qish (TTS) dvigateli orqali bo'ladi — u faqat kursdagi inglizcha so'zni oladi.

Xabarlaringiz to'g'ridan-to'g'ri Telegram'ga emas, developer boshqaradigan oraliq xizmat (Cloudflare Worker) orqali yetkaziladi — bu xizmat xabarlarni saqlamaydi, faqat Telegram'ga uzatadi (faqat promo kod tekshiruvida kodning ishlatilgani va anonim qurilma identifikatori saqlanadi). Shu sababli yetkazishda Cloudflare va Telegram'ning o'z maxfiylik siyosatlari ham qo'llaniladi. Ilova reklama ko'rsatmaydi va hech qanday tahlil/kuzatuv (analytics) vositalaridan foydalanmaydi.

Ma'lumotlarni uchinchi shaxslar bilan baham ko'rish

Biz hech qanday ma'lumotingizni sotmaymiz va ijaraga bermaymiz. Developerga yetib boradigan yagona ma'lumotlar — siz o'zingiz yuborgan «Muammo haqida xabar berish» matni, rozilik bergan texnik xatolik (crash) hisoboti, ingliz mashqi hisoboti, ixtiyoriy hissa izohi hamda promo kod tekshiruvi; ular developer boshqaradigan oraliq xizmat (Cloudflare) orqali ishlanadi/Telegram'ga yetkaziladi va faqat siz tanlaganingizda sodir bo'ladi. Bundan tashqari, o'z siyosati bilan ishlaydigan tashqi xizmatlar: Google Play (yangilanishlar, Plus obunasi va ixtiyoriy hissa to'lovlari — Google'ga biz hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot bermaymiz, to'lov ma'lumotlarini esa olmaymiz) va telefoningizning nutqni tanish xizmati (ixtiyoriy gapirish mashqlarida ovozni internet orqali qayta ishlashi mumkin; ovoz developerga yuborilmaydi).

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Barcha ma'lumotlar qurilmangizda saqlangani uchun, Ilovani o'chirib tashlaganingizda (uninstall) unga tegishli barcha ma'lumotlar — jumladan motivatsion video nusxalari va qadamlar tarixi — qurilmadan butunlay o'chiriladi. Ilova ichida ham: Sozlamalar → «Barcha ma'lumotlarni o'chirish» hamma narsani tozalaydi; alohida yozuvlarni (vazifa, odat, video, maktub) o'z bo'limida o'chirasiz; qadam sanagichni o'chirsangiz uning yozuvi ham darhol yo'qoladi. Bizda hech qanday nusxa qolmaydi — bundan istisno faqat siz ataylab yuborgan xabarlar (muammo xabari, xatolik hisoboti, mashq hisoboti, ixtiyoriy hissa izohi) va ishlatilgan promo kod yozuvi (ularni o'chirishni so'rash uchun 10-bo'limdagi manzillarga yozing). Plus obunasi Google Play hisobingizga bog'liq — uni Google Play'ning «Obunalar» bo'limida boshqarasiz. Telefoningizda Google zaxirasi yoqilgan bo'lsa, Android ilova ma'lumotining nusxasini Google akkauntingizda saqlashi mumkin. Buni telefon sozlamalarida o'chirish mumkin.

Xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlaringizning deyarli barchasi qurilmangizdan tashqariga chiqmaydi, shu sabab ularning xavfsizligi asosan qurilmangizning o'z himoyasiga (ekran qulfi, barmoq izi va h.k.) bog'liq. Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Unquit" application (Android and Windows versions; the "App") handles user information. Unquit helps you manage daily tasks, habits, goals, notes, letters to the future, your streak, focus tools, motivation videos and English lessons.

In short

Unquit keeps almost all of your data only on your device; there is no account, no ads and no analytics. Something leaves your device over the internet only when YOU take action: (1) a message you send via 'Report a problem', (2) a technical error report if you agree to the prompt shown after a crash, (3) an English exercise report if you tap the flag button (including your answer and, in speaking exercises, the recognized speech text), (4) an optional name and note you submit in 'Contribute' — all forwarded to the developer through Telegram via a Cloudflare Worker; and (5) when you redeem a one-time promo code, the code and an anonymous device identifier are verified by that service. Besides that, three external services work under their own policies: Google Play (the Plus subscription, optional contributions, update checks), your device's speech recognition service (the optional speaking exercises in the English section — it may process your voice over the internet) and the mail or Telegram app whose link you tap yourself. Unquit does not record, store or receive your voice.

Information we do NOT collect

The App does not request or collect:

  • name, phone number or email address (except an optional name you choose to write in 'Contribute' — section 4);
  • account registration or login (no account is required);
  • location (GPS) data;
  • voice recordings;
  • contacts, photos or other personal files (except the motivation video you pick yourself — section 2);
  • payment card details (payments are handled by Google Play — section 4).

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter into the App, and everything the App computes for you, is stored solely on your device and is not sent to any server of the developer:

  • tasks, habits, goal maps, notes, letters to the future, promise contracts, statistics and settings;
  • the streak, ice reserve, badges and weekly recaps;
  • if the step counter is on — the daily step count read from the phone's sensor (a number only, no location);
  • your progress in the English section (learned words, review schedule, XP);
  • a copy of the video file you picked for a motivation video (in the App's own folder; deleting the video deletes the copy);
  • the app password and the security question and answer (on the device only; we do not know them and cannot recover them);
  • the state of the Plus subscription and promo code (confirmation cache).

A data backup (export) is created only when you ask for it and is written where you choose (clipboard or a file) — where you keep it and whom you give it to is up to you.

The only exceptions are items sent strictly by your own action as listed in section 4 (problem report, crash report, exercise report, optional contribution note and promo code check).

If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your Google account. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

App permissions

The App uses the following permissions (Android):

  • Notifications — to show tasks, habits, notes and letters at the scheduled time, the 'Streak at risk' and weekly recap messages, and to keep background services (focus session, step counter, wake alarm) visible;
  • Exact alarms & reminders — so reminders and the morning wake alarm fire at precisely the right time;
  • Wake alarm & lock screen (full-screen intent, wake lock and alarm playback foreground service) — only if you turn on the morning wake alarm, to wake the lock screen and play the alarm sound at the scheduled time; you may optionally be asked to exempt the app from battery optimization (no personal data is collected);
  • Run on boot — so scheduled reminders and the wake alarm are not lost when the device restarts;
  • Vibration — to vibrate on notifications, wake alarms and button taps;
  • Physical activity — requested only if you turn on automatic step counting for a habit: the step count is read from the phone's sensor and stored on the device; a background service stays connected to the sensor. Location is not used;
  • Usage access and Display over other apps — only if you turn on the 'Block apps' tool: during a block the App checks which app is currently open and draws a barrier over a chosen app. Which apps were opened is never stored or sent anywhere; the list of installed apps is read only so you can pick the ones to block;
  • Do Not Disturb access — only when Focus Shield is on: it silences the phone during a task timer and restores it afterwards;
  • Microphone — only in the speaking exercises of the English section, when you tap the microphone button: your speech is recognized by your phone's speech recognition service (e.g. Google) — on the device when possible, otherwise on that service's servers under its own privacy policy. The App does not record, store, receive or share your voice; the recognized text is used to check your answer on the phone and is sent only if you explicitly submit an exercise error report. You can turn the exercises off in the section menu or revoke the permission in the phone settings;
  • Network state — to tell the cause of an error in the speaking exercise ('no internet'); it sends nothing;
  • Internet — only for (a) a 'Report a problem' message, (b) an error report you agreed to, (c) an English exercise report you submit, (d) an optional contribution note, (e) verifying a one-time promo code and (f) Google Play update checks, the Plus subscription and optional contribution payments.

A motivation video is chosen through the phone's own picker — no gallery permission is requested, the App receives only the single file you pick.

Apart from the internet, the microphone (as described above, your phone's speech service may send audio to its servers) and the Google Play cases, no permission is used to collect or transmit your personal data.

The Windows version has no permission system: data is stored in the App's folder on your computer; speaking exercises use Windows' own speech recognition service (whether it recognizes online depends on your Windows settings).

Internet and third-party services

The App works mostly offline — all fonts, the course and the content are bundled inside the App, and no internet is required for everyday use.

The internet is used only in these cases:

  • If you send a message via 'Report a problem' — your text, together with the time it was sent, the app language and version and your device type (e.g. Android), is sent to the developer through the Telegram service. This happens only when you tap 'Send'; if there is no internet, the message is not sent and stays on your device until the internet is on.
  • If the app crashes unexpectedly — on the next launch we ask whether to send a technical error report (device type, system version and the error trace; without your personal content). It is sent only when you tap 'Send' and the internet is on; if you are offline the report stays on your device, and if you decline it is deleted.
  • If you tap the flag button in an English exercise to report an issue — the exercise ID, prompt, your answer (in speaking exercises, the recognized speech text), selected reason and app/OS version are sent to the developer via Telegram. This happens only when you submit; if you are offline, the report waits in a local queue on your device.
  • If you optionally enter a name and note after a payment in 'Contribute' — that name, note and tier are sent to the developer via Telegram.
  • If you redeem a one-time promo code — the code and an anonymous device identifier (ANDROID_ID on Android, MachineGuid on Windows) are verified and stored by the developer's Cloudflare Worker to prevent reuse and allow restoration on the same device.
  • If you yourself tap an email or Telegram link — the relevant external app (your mail app or Telegram) opens.
  • If the App was installed from Google Play — when the App opens, it checks via Google Play whether a new version is available and, if you consent, downloads the update in the background. None of your personal data is sent to Google Play; this relies on Google Play's standard update mechanism, to which Google's own privacy policy applies.
  • The Unquit Plus subscription (monthly, 3-month or yearly) and the optional payment in the 'Contribute' section are processed through Google Play. Payment and card details are handled only by Google — neither the App nor the developer sees them; the App receives from Google Play only the 'subscription active / not active' state and caches it on the device. Google Play's own terms and privacy policy apply to this process.
  • If you use the optional speaking exercises in the English section — your voice is recognized by your phone's speech recognition service (e.g. Google). The App asks it to work offline, but whether the audio leaves the phone is decided by that service: if offline English is not installed (and sometimes even if it is), it may send your voice over the internet to its own servers under its own privacy policy. The voice is not sent to the developer, and the App neither records nor stores it. The exercises work only after you grant the microphone permission, and they can be turned off in the section menu. Reading words aloud (pronunciation) uses the phone's own text-to-speech engine — it receives only the English word from the course.

Your messages are delivered not directly to Telegram but through an intermediate service operated by the developer (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards messages without storing them (only promo code redemptions store the redeemed code state and anonymous device identifier). Delivery is therefore also subject to Cloudflare's and Telegram's own privacy policies. The App shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell or rent any of your data. The only items that reach the developer are a message you send via 'Report a problem', a technical error (crash) report you agree to send, an English exercise report you submit, an optional contribution note and a promo code check; they are processed/forwarded via an intermediate service operated by the developer (Cloudflare) to Telegram, and only when you choose to. Beyond that, external services operating under their own policies: Google Play (updates, the Plus subscription and optional contribution payments — we give Google no personal data and receive no payment details) and your device's speech recognition service (it may process your voice over the internet in the optional speaking exercises; the voice is not sent to the developer).

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. It is intended for a general audience.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, uninstalling the App permanently removes all of its data — including motivation video copies and the step history — from your device. Inside the App as well: Settings → 'Delete all data' clears everything; individual records (a task, habit, video, letter) are deleted in their own section; turning the step counter off removes its record immediately. We keep no copy — the only exceptions are items you deliberately sent (problem reports, crash reports, exercise reports, optional contribution notes) and redeemed promo code records (write to the addresses in section 10 to ask for their deletion). The Plus subscription is tied to your Google Play account — you manage it in Google Play's 'Subscriptions' section. If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your Google account. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Security

Since almost all of your data never leaves your device, its security depends mainly on your device's own protections (screen lock, fingerprint, etc.). The 4-digit password inside the App is an extra lock — it is stored only on the device. The export file (backup) is not encrypted: keep it in a safe place.
